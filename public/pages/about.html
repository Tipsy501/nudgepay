About NudgePay

NudgePay was born from a simple observation: freelancers spend too much time on overhead.

Our mission is to provide global freelancers and small businesses with professional tools that are fast, secure, and entirely free.

We believe that creating an invoice shouldn't require a monthly subscription or a complex account setup. NudgePay is a "zero-friction" generator. We don't store your data on our servers; every PDF is generated locally in your browser to ensure maximum privacy and security for your business information.

Whether you're a designer in London, a developer in Cape Town, or a writer in Tokyo, NudgePay is designed to help you get paid faster with professional documentation that works across borders.

NudgePay is and will remain free forever. We are supported by our community and light advertising to keep the servers running and the tools evolving.
